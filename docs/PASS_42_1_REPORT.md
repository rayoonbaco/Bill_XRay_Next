# PASS 42.1 - REALITY STACK

## Goal
Move Bill X-Ray beyond statute-only interpretation without corrupting provenance.

A policy claim now travels through separately labeled evidence lanes:

1. RHETORIC - what the public was told.
2. ENACTED_LAW - what Congress actually enacted.
3. IMPLEMENTATION - how agencies converted the law into operating rules.
4. COURT_INTERPRETATION - what courts materially changed or clarified, when loaded.
5. OFFICIAL_ANALYSIS - what official reviewers such as GAO/CBO/CRS found.
6. OBSERVED_OUTCOME - what later program or market evidence shows.
7. SECONDARY_INTERPRETATION - journalism, scholarship, advocacy, and criticism, held subordinate to primary evidence and not yet activated in this first gate.

## Stress test
ACA keep-your-doctor claim.

Pass 42 established that the political promise was broader than the statutory guarantee. Pass 42.1 adds the implementation and outcome layers. CMS network-adequacy rules regulate sufficient access; they do not create a right to one named physician. Later GAO work documented narrow-network access concerns and issuer network-narrowing behavior. Those later facts test the promise in practice, but they are not silently rewritten into the original statute and do not prove one simple causal chain.

## Critical guardrail
**A later fact may challenge a political promise without becoming something Congress literally wrote.**

Likewise:
- regulation is not statute;
- court interpretation is not legislative text;
- GAO analysis is not implementation;
- observed market behavior is not automatic statutory causation;
- commentary is not primary evidence.

## Result
The architecture can now answer a more honest question:

> What they said -> what Congress wrote -> what agencies did -> what courts said (if relevant evidence is loaded) -> what official reviewers found -> what happened.

The output remains layered and provenance-aware rather than collapsing into TRUE/FALSE.
