# Pass 45.1 - The Transformation Ladder

Goal: show one real statutory provision changing from raw Congressional language into human meaning while preserving every important legal distinction and receipt.

The public article remains primary. `HOW THIS BECAME HUMAN` opens a separate full-screen demonstration. The example uses CHIPS Section 102(a)(2)(B) because the provision contains two large dollar figures with different legal nouns: program cost and loan principal.

The ladder stages are: raw bill, grammar, atomic facts, restored nouns, Truth Court, human idea, finished sentence, receipt.

Design rule: this is a cinematic explanatory page, not an agent dashboard.
