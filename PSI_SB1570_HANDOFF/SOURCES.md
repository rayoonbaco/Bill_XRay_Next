The enacted law—not summaries or the introduced bill—controls this handoff. P.A. 103-0491 is the foundation. P.A. 104-0395 supplies the current municipal selection amendment, effective July 1, 2025. The municipal regime begins at 65 ILCS 5/Art. 11 Div. 39.2; the school regime begins at 105 ILCS 5/Art. 15A. External owner records have not been used to claim adoption or demand. Those must be collected project by project.
