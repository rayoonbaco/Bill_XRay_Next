# PASS 41 - Editorial Intelligence Room

## Goal
Turn the broad, court-cleared TCJA meaning substrate into an original human editorial without allowing style to invent facts.

## Architecture
Pass 41 creates two new layers:

1. `editorial_room.py` selects the facts that define the story and proposes synthesis, analogy, and restrained wit.
2. `editorial_story.py` writes the essay while maintaining a sentence ledger. Every factual or editorial sentence lists the cleared facts that support it.

## Editorial team
Editor-in-Chief, Humanist, Investigative Editor, Minimalist, Analogy Writer, Satirist, Skeptic, Citation Referee.

## Doctrine
**Style may illuminate proved meaning. Style may not create new facts.**

The public voice is original Bill X-Ray prose. It does not imitate a named writer.

## Human gate
The engine can prove provenance and guardrails. Ray must decide whether the essay is actually worth reading.
