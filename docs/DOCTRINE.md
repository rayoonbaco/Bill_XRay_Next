# Bill X-Ray Doctrine

## Mission
Bill X-Ray exists to give ordinary people intellectual access to legislation they otherwise could not realistically decode. It does not tell citizens what to think. It earns them the ability to think about the law for themselves.

## Human-AI operating doctrine
1. Be stubborn about the purpose; flexible about the form.
2. The human protects mission, taste, lived judgment, and the final meaning gate. The AI team owns exploration, architecture, synthesis, challenge, and implementation.
3. Human implementation requests are hypotheses, not sacred requirements. If a requested screen, card, workflow, or code path weakens the mission, the AI team should challenge it rather than obediently optimize the wrong abstraction.
4. AI can be part of original human thought. When a source is too complex, coded, technical, or inaccessible for a person to understand directly, AI translation may be what makes an informed human opinion possible in the first place.
5. Therefore the system must expand human agency, not replace it: decode, reconstruct, expose uncertainty, show receipts, and return judgment to the citizen.

## Truth and translation doctrine
6. Complexity may remain backstage. It may not leak into the citizen experience.
7. Translation means reconstructing what actually happened, not shortening statutory text.
8. Every material factual statement must be traceable to evidence.
9. Humor is permitted only when it increases understanding and survives factual review.
10. Strange does not mean corrupt. Ask before accusing.
11. Political claims and statutory mechanisms are separate evidence lanes.
12. If context is unresolved, say so. Never fill a semantic gap with confidence theater.
13. The final public work should read like gifted human editorial prose, not an AI dashboard.
14. Human judgment remains the mission gate. Green tests do not make cryptic prose acceptable.
15. A sentence that is clever but does not clarify is cut. A sentence that clarifies but cannot be proven is cut. A sentence that is accurate but still sounds like Congress is rewritten.

## Release doctrine
16. Full-file replacements are preferred over surgical patch packages for Bill X-Ray Next.
17. Every pass must be runnable from the main project folder. One-click BAT files and smoke-cast files live in that folder.
18. The smoke cast must tell the human exactly what the pass changed, what was tested, what still remains uncertain, and what—if anything—the human must do.
19. CLEAR means the exact packaged project passed its automated gates. NEEDS REVIEW means stop and read the reason shown on screen.
20. Simplicity in the public experience is earned by rigor underneath, never by hiding uncertainty or discarding evidence.

## Claim-vs-law doctrine added at Pass 42
21. Public rhetoric and enacted law are different evidence objects. Never search a slogan in the statute and call that fact-checking.
22. Decompose a public claim into the legal promises that would need to be guaranteed for the statement to be literally supported by the law.
23. A statutory mechanism may support, qualify, narrow, or fail to establish a public claim. Those are different outcomes.
24. Do not force TRUE/FALSE when the honest answer is conditional, incomplete, or outside the four corners of the statute.
25. A mismatch between rhetoric and statutory machinery is not evidence of motive, deception, corruption, or later real-world harm.

## Reality-stack doctrine added at Pass 42.1
26. Enacted law is only one layer of policy reality. Keep rhetoric, statute, regulation, court interpretation, official analysis, observed outcomes, and secondary commentary as separate evidence objects.
27. Later implementation may explain how a law operated; it may not be silently rewritten into what Congress literally enacted.
28. Later outcomes may test a public promise; they do not automatically prove that the statute caused the outcome.
29. Source authority is hierarchical and typed. Primary law and official implementation outrank commentary for claims about what the law or government actually did.
30. A missing evidence lane means not loaded, not nonexistent. Bill X-Ray must name the gap instead of pretending completeness.
31. When reliable sources conflict, preserve the conflict and provenance. Do not average disagreement into fake certainty.
