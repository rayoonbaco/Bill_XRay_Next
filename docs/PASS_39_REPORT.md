# PASS 39 - Adversarial Truth Court

## Goal
Prevent investigation signals from becoming editorial claims before the evidence earns them.

## Court roles
Each candidate receives:
- prosecutor / concern case
- ordinary defense / innocent explanation
- progressive reading
- conservative reading
- text referee
- citation referee

## Verdicts
- `SUPPORTED_NARROWLY`: only an already-verified atomic meaning event may support a limited factual statement.
- `QUESTION_ONLY`: the evidence supports asking the question, not asserting the implied answer.
- `HOLD`: the evidence packet itself is insufficient.

## Doctrine
The court is asymmetric by design. It is allowed to remove claims much more easily than it is allowed to create them.

The future Human Story Engine may receive court-cleared statements and questions. It may not silently promote unresolved implications into facts.
