# Owner Authority and Ethical Boundaries

- The Act expressly covers Illinois municipalities and school districts through separate statutory regimes. Do not silently extend these rules to counties, park districts, state agencies, or other bodies.
- Authorization is optional. Traditional and other lawful delivery approaches remain possible.
- A professional preparing statutory scope and performance criteria cannot join the proposing design-build entity for that project.
- A pecuniary interest, relationship, or circumstance that creates advantage or the appearance of impropriety can bar consideration.
- Previous awards under an applicable Illinois public-procurement statute do not, by themselves, disqualify an entity under the current municipal selection provision. P.A. 104-0395 added that municipal sentence effective July 1, 2025. Municipal and school pathways remain legally separate.

Current municipal law checked through [P.A. 104-0395](https://www.ilga.gov/Legislation/PublicActs/PrinterFriendly/104-0395), using [P.A. 103-0491](https://www.ilga.gov/Legislation/PublicActs/PrinterFriendly/103-0491) as the foundation act.
- Early education and relationship development must remain lawful, transparent, and outside prohibited scope preparation or unfair advantage. PSI cannot decide fact-specific compliance.
- Route project-specific contact, lobbying, gifts, conflicts, procurement communications, and team-eligibility questions to qualified Illinois counsel and the owner's published rules.
