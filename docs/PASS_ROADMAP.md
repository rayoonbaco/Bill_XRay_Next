# Finish-Line Pass Strategy

## PASS 35 — Legislative Decompiler
Build a structured meaning substrate: actor, action, target, before, after, timing, conditions, uncertainty, receipts. Prove it on TCJA. Refuse false certainty.

## PASS 36 — Before / After Reconstruction
Resolve amended external-code context so changes can be stated as human before/after events, not statutory commands.

## PASS 37 — Congressional Rosetta Grammar
Create a reusable grammar of legislative constructs (strike/insert, notwithstanding, subject to, deemed, treated as, promulgate, effective date, appropriations, authorizations, tax mechanics) with context requirements and safe human transformations.

## PASS 38 — Investigation Engine
Rank what is genuinely important or revealing: money, authority, carve-outs, contradictions, unusual specificity, omissions, and mismatches between stated purpose and operative mechanics.

## PASS 39 — Adversarial Truth Court
Require provocative observations to survive prosecutor, defense, left lens, right lens, text referee, and citation referee review.

## PASS 40 — Human Story Engine
Generate one coherent essay from verified meaning events. No cards required. No humor requirement. The essay must pass a human comprehension gate.

## PASS 41 — Editorial Intelligence Room
Add original common-man editorial intelligence: clarity, analogy, understatement, irony, human consequence, and restrained satire. Kill any joke that does not illuminate.

## PASS 41.1 — The Columnist
Force editorial selection harder: find one evidence-backed thesis, omit most available facts, and write a column with narrative pull rather than marching through evidence categories. First-three-paragraph hook gate required.

## PASS 42 — Claim vs. Law Mapper
Map public political promises and talking points to the legal mechanisms that would need to support them. Separate what the law guarantees, permits, conditions, delegates, or never says.

## PASS 42.1 — Reality Stack
Connect rhetoric, enacted law, implementation, court interpretation, official analysis, observed outcomes, and later commentary without blending their authority or causation.

## PASS 43 — Generalization Trial
Freeze the TCJA engine and run ACA, IRA, and OBBBA without bill-specific prose hacks. Failure to generalize is a release failure.

## PASS 44 — Public Product Inversion
Make the essay the house and the forensic machinery the basement. Primary experience: “I READ THE BILL — Here’s what it actually does.” Secondary action: “Check my homework.”

## PASS 45 — Fresh Bill A-Grade Trial
Add a fourth law that was not part of the original public trio and grade the public essay/homework like a teacher. The release fails unless the new story preserves legal nouns, traces every paragraph to official receipts, translates the law into ordinary language, and earns at least 93/100 for evidence, comprehension, synthesis, and restraint.

## PASS 46 — Visual Satirist
Generate one evidence-grounded editorial illustration that compresses the central contradiction or defining mechanism into a memorable visual metaphor. No infographic clutter.
