# Start Here for PSI

Bill X-Ray uses Illinois SB 1570, Public Act 103-0491, effective January 1, 2024, as the foundation act—not the introduced bill. It adds municipal design-build authority at 65 ILCS 5/Art. 11 Div. 39.2 and school design-build authority at 105 ILCS 5/Art. 15A. Municipal selection law is checked through Public Act 104-0395, effective July 1, 2025, which adds a discretionary municipal single-response pathway while preserving the ordinary two-to-six shortlist.

Official sources: [foundation P.A. 103-0491](https://www.ilga.gov/Legislation/PublicActs/PrinterFriendly/103-0491) and [current amendment P.A. 104-0395](https://www.ilga.gov/Legislation/PublicActs/PrinterFriendly/104-0395).

## Evidence classes

- **Verified statutory facts:** rules in `STATUTORY_RULES.json`, supported by the Illinois General Assembly sources in `SOURCES.md`.
- **Derived facts:** procurement sequence and evidence dependencies reconstructed from multiple provisions. These are labeled `DERIVED`.
- **Commercial/investigative hypotheses:** possible earlier public traces and search strategies. These are labeled `HYPOTHESIS`; they are not promises of a project or statements of law.
- **Unknowns:** adoption, funding, owner intent, project suitability, competitive position, and the legal effect of particular early contacts.

## What PSI should implement

Treat the Act as opportunity-discovery grammar: identify an eligible owner; reconstruct a documented capital need; score funding, planning, professional-services, delivery-method, and procurement signals separately; preserve dates and sources; and show Tom why the evidence justifies WATCH, INVESTIGATE, BUILD RELATIONSHIP, VERIFY DELIVERY PATH, VERIFY FUNDING, PREPARE QUALIFICATIONS, or TOO LATE.

Do not convert “owner may use design-build” into “owner will use design-build.” Do not recommend helping prepare statutory scope/performance criteria while expecting to join the proposal team. Escalate conflict, impropriety, lobbying, gift, and procurement-contact questions to qualified Illinois counsel.
