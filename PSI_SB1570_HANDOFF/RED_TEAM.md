# Commercial Thesis Red Team

- **Authorization is not adoption.** Owners can continue using other methods.
- **New authority is not new money.** Every project still needs need, approval, and funding.
- **Signals can lie.** A study may be shelved; a capital plan may be aspirational; a grant may fail; an RFQ may serve a different delivery path.
- **The pre-RFP window is not a license to shape a procurement.** Scope preparation and advantageous relationships can create disqualification risk.
- **The addressable owner set is bounded.** Do not generalize municipal/school rules to every Illinois public entity.
- **A qualifications-weighted process is not automatically a PSI advantage.** Performance Services must document every required qualification and assemble the right team.
- **The statute alone cannot prove commercial fit.** PSI needs real owner records, Performance Services capability evidence, competition, project economics, and counsel-reviewed boundaries.

The strategy becomes commercially useless if PSI merely scrapes RFPs, floods Tom with single-signal leads, hides missing evidence, or ranks opportunities without conflict and owner-authority checks.
