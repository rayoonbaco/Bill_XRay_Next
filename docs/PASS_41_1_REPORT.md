# PASS 41.1 - THE COLUMNIST

## Goal
Stop explaining TCJA by evidence category. Find one evidence-backed column with a thesis strong enough to organize the law for a normal reader.

## Diagnosis from Pass 41
Pass 41 was clear, accurate, and readable, but its structure still exposed the internal evidence bins: household rules, family credits, business rules, corporate rules, estates, and Opportunity Zones. The reader could feel the 27 selected facts underneath the prose.

## Architecture move
Pass 41.1 does not weaken the semantic or Truth Court layers. It adds a harsher editorial gate above them.

The columnist receives 33 court-cleared facts and deliberately selects only 14. The other 19 remain available in the receipt system but do not enter the column merely because they are true.

## Column thesis
The revealing thing about TCJA is not one tax rate. It is how often a simple public headline turns into a different rule once the statute's clocks, conditions, and gatekeepers are put back in.

## New gates
- One thesis, not a category tour.
- No more than 15 selected facts.
- At least half of available facts must be omitted.
- First three paragraphs must be under 260 words and create a hook.
- Every paragraph must list supporting cleared fact IDs.
- No motive, corruption, partisan, causal, or distributional inference beyond the packet.
- Final column target: 650-1000 words.

## Human acceptance test
After paragraph three: **Do I want paragraph four?**

After the final paragraph: **Did this feel like one revealing column rather than an organized tax summary?**
