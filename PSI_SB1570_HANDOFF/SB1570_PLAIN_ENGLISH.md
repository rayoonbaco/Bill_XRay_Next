# SB 1570 in Plain English

The law lets Illinois municipalities and school districts choose design-build, where one entity is responsible under one contract for both design and construction. Once an owner chooses it, the owner must announce the coming solicitation online at least 14 days before the RFP; include schedule, budget, funding, qualifications, contract, performance, and scoring information; use qualified professional help to define the project; and follow the prescribed evaluation process.

Phase I centers on qualifications and excludes price. Phase II examines technical and cost proposals, with total project cost weighted no more than 30%. The ordinary municipal shortlist contains two to six firms. Under Public Act 104-0395, effective July 1, 2025, a municipality receiving only one Phase I response may evaluate that single respondent in Phase II if the municipality, in its discretion, finds proceeding to be in its best interest. Municipal and school pathways remain legally separate; the single-response option is not a school-only distinction. Smaller projects may combine phases while retaining every required evaluation. The selected entity later reports on specified utilization goals.

Current law checked through P.A. 104-0395. Sources: [foundation P.A. 103-0491](https://www.ilga.gov/Legislation/PublicActs/PrinterFriendly/103-0491) and [current amendment P.A. 104-0395](https://www.ilga.gov/Legislation/PublicActs/PrinterFriendly/104-0395).

This is legal authorization, not evidence that a named owner has selected design-build or that a project exists.
